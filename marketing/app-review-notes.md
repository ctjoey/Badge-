2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Nuke30 is a personal reminder tool for people who travel between U.S. nuclear power plants for refueling outages: contract craft workers, technicians, engineers, and supervisors who work for many different employers.
At U.S. nuclear plants, if a worker is away from a site for more than 30 days, their badge access lapses and they must redo the full in-processing (background check, training, drug test). Workers often forget the date they left a site. Nuke30 counts the days since they left each site, shows how many days remain, and sends iPhone reminders before the 30 days run out. It also lists upcoming refueling outages so workers can plan their next job.

3. HOW TO USE IT (no login or sample files needed)
- Open the app. On first launch the Status tab says "Pick your sites" – tap Open Settings.
- In Settings, use the plant list to pick one or more plants (for example "Turkey Point" and "Vogtle").
- Pick reminder days (for example 7 and 3 days before). Tap Allow alerts, then Send test alert to see a notification.
- Go to the Status tab. Each plant has a card. Tap "30 Day Badge Reminder" and pick a past date: the card shows the days left and a 30-day bar. "On Site" means the worker is still working there.
- Outages tab: shows current and upcoming refueling outages. Tap the star to follow a plant, or the pencil / "Add / Update" to enter your own dates.
- If a site's 30 days have passed, the card shows EXPIRED with a short cartoon animation.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app makes no network requests. All data stays on the device (local storage). There are no accounts, analytics, ads, payment processors, or AI services. Reminders use Apple's local notifications on the device.
The outage list built into the app comes from the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission's public daily "Power Reactor Status Reports" (public U.S. government information at nrc.gov). It is updated when we release a new app version.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in every region. Its content covers U.S. nuclear plants because the 30-day rule is a U.S. rule.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY
Nuke30 is an independent personal reminder. It is not affiliated with the NRC or any utility. It does not grant, check, or change anyone's site access, and it does not connect to any plant system. It only uses public information: the 30-day rule comes from the public federal regulation 10 CFR 73.56, and the outage data comes from public NRC reports.

Guideline 3.2: the app is not for one business or its employees. It is for the general public of nuclear outage workers, who are mostly independent contractors working for many different companies across many utilities.
