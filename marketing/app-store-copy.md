## Description (4000)

```
Nuke30 is the badge clock for nuclear outage workers.

When you out-process from a plant, a 30-day clock starts on your unescorted access (UA). In-process again before it runs out and getting your badge back is simpler. Miss it and there is more to redo. Nuke30 keeps that clock in front of you for every plant you work.

YOUR BADGE CLOCK
• One card for every plant you pick, from all 55 U.S. nuclear power plant sites
• Tap "On Site" while you're working there
• Tap "30 Day Badge Reminder" when you out-process, and set the date
• See the days you have left and the date you must in-process by
• Green, amber and red so you know at a glance how close it is

ALERTS BEFORE IT EXPIRES
• Pick any reminder days from 1 to 15 days before your window closes
• Always get a last-day alert at 8:00 AM
• Tap an alert to jump straight to that plant

OUTAGES, 5 YEARS OUT
• Current and upcoming refueling outages for every unit
• See only your plants, or every plant in the country
• Star the outages you want to follow
• Know a better date? Update it, or add an outage that's missing

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up, no ads
• Everything you enter stays on your phone
• Nuke30 never asks for badge numbers, Social Security numbers or anything about site security

AND A LITTLE FUN
• Let a badge expire and see what happens when you open the app.

ABOUT THE OUTAGE DATES
Utilities don't publish exact outage schedules. Nuke30 projects them from the NRC's public daily reactor power reports and each unit's refueling cycle. Treat them as good estimates and go by your call-out for exact dates.

HELP AND OUTAGE UPDATES
Questions, or know an exact outage date? Email Nuke30app@outlook.com.

IMPORTANT
Nuke30 is a personal planning aid. It is not an official access determination and is not affiliated with the NRC, any utility or any staffing company. Your site's access authorization program has the final say.
```

## App Review notes (4000)

```
Nuke30 needs no account or login.

To try it:
1. Open Settings, tap "My power plants", tick two or three plants, tap Done.
2. Open Status. On one plant tap "30 Day Badge Reminder" and pick a date about 28 days ago. The card shows 2 days left. iOS asks for notification permission the first time; the app schedules local notifications only (no push, no server).
3. Settings > "Send test alert" delivers a local notification in 5 seconds.
4. Open Outages to see projected refueling outages. These are calculated from the U.S. NRC's public daily Power Reactor Status reports and ship inside the app.
5. Set a plant's date to 31 or more days ago and reopen the app: a short cartoon mushroom-cloud animation plays with the message "Badge expired". It is a lighthearted reminder for nuclear power plant workers; tap anywhere to close it. With Reduce Motion on it shows only the message.

The app collects no data. Everything is stored on the device. It is a planning aid for nuclear power plant outage workers and is not affiliated with the NRC or any utility.
```
