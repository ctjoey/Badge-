Common questions

How do I start my 30-day clock?
On the Status tab, tap 30 Day Badge Reminder on the plant's card and set the date you out-processed.

I'm going in and out of the protected area. What do I pick?
Keep On Site selected. Switch to 30 Day Badge Reminder only when you out-process and your badge is deactivated.

I'm not getting alerts.
Open Settings in Nuke30 and tap Send test alert. If nothing arrives, open the iPhone Settings app → Notifications → Nuke30 and turn on Allow Notifications. Alerts are only scheduled for plants set to 30 Day Badge Reminder.

An outage date is wrong.
Utilities don't publish exact schedules, so the dates are projections. On the Outages tab, tap the pencil on an outage to enter the dates you know, or tap Add / Update to add one.

How do I remove a plant?
Tap Remove from Status on its card, or untick it in Settings.

Does Nuke30 share my information?
No. Everything stays on your phone. See the privacy policy.

Nuke30 is a personal planning aid. It is not an official access determination and is not affiliated with the NRC, any utility or any staffing company.
