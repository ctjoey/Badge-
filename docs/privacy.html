Privacy Policy — Nuke30

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Nuke30 is a badge-clock and outage-planning app for nuclear power plant outage workers. The short version: Nuke30 does not collect any data about you.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter in Nuke30 is stored only on your device:

  • the plants you pick,
  • your badge status and badge-out dates,
  • your reminder days,
  • outages you follow, add or correct.

None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. There is no account, no sign-up and no cloud sync. If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it.

What we collect

Nothing. Nuke30 has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not use any third-party services that collect data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Nuke30 schedules reminders on your own phone. They are created and delivered by your device. No server is involved.

Sharing

The Share button opens your phone's share sheet with a short description of the app. Nothing about you or your badge is included.

Outage dates

The outage schedule inside the app is calculated from the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission's public daily reactor status reports. It is the same for every user and contains no personal information.

Information you should not enter

Nuke30 never asks for badge numbers, Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, badge photos or site security details. Please do not type them into the app.

Children

Nuke30 is a work tool for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
